Lord of my Life

A personal planner built around how research actually goes: the long project timelines, the day-to-day tasks underneath them, and the focus sessions you get them done in.

  • Work and Personal — tasks under sections you define, with due dates and subtasks.
  • Gantt chart — projects broken into dated phases, and every deadline on one timeline.
  • Session — a pomodoro timer with a queue, showing what time you'll finish.
  • Calendar — a week of events with your planned focus sessions drawn over it.
  • Budget — fixed costs, spend-down budgets, and one-click presets.
